---

## Section Descriptions

- **Dashboard Overview:** Entry point with motivational info and a quick start button.
- **Practice Routines:** Choose, create, or revisit structured routines; see steps and progress.
- **Chord & Technique Practice:** Interactive chord diagrams and technique drills.
- **Ear Training Tool:** Fun ear training games and exercises.
- **Play Along Mode:** Practice with real music, tabs, and backing tracks.
- **Progress Tracking:** Visualize growth, streaks, and achievements.
- **Customization & AI Coach:** Personalize the experience and get smart practice advice.

**Note:**
- Each box/section represents a major UI area or card.
